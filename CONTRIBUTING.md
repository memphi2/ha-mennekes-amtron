# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through pull requests.

- **License.** Contributions are accepted under the project's
  [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), as its section 5 describes. There is no
  contributor license agreement.
- **No vendor material.** Do not submit vendor documents, firmware,
  configuration-tool files, device backups, or data from a real device such
  as serial numbers, addresses or credentials.
- **Working on the code.** [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) describes the structure,
  the commands, the tests and the safety rules that every change keeps.
- **Security.** Report a vulnerability privately as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
  describes, not in a public issue.
